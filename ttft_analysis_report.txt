──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STATISTICAL NOTES
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

• P95 (95th percentile): 95% of requests complete within this time
• Standard Error: Precision of the mean estimate (±SE)
• Lower std dev = more consistent/predictable performance
• TTFT measures time until FIRST token (not total response time)

• Data based on Artificial Analysis benchmarks and provider documentation
• P95 values estimated where not explicitly published
• For production use, conduct benchmarks with your specific workload
